❓ How to play

Each season runs 30 simulated trading days on six fictional coins, each with its own volatility — STBL barely moves, RUG is a rollercoaster that trends down more often than not. Buy low with the Buy buttons (a share of your cash), sell high with the Sell buttons (a share of your holdings), then press Advance day to move the market forward — prices drift and wobble randomly, and some days bring a news event that swings one coin harder in one direction. At the end of day 30 your season scores on total portfolio value (cash plus holdings), converting your result into insight, spent in the Research tab on permanent upgrades: more starting cash, calmer markets, advance warning of event days, and rewinds to undo a bad day. Daily Challenge gives everyone the same 30-day market for the day, once per day, with a streak for playing. Every price path is generated right here in your browser from a seed — nothing is pulled from real markets.
